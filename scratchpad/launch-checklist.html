🚀 Before ATBP takes a real order

What's left to launch.

The product is done. What's missing now is everything outside the codebase — a real payment processor, a domain, someone to answer a support email. Grouped by how soon each one actually blocks you, with where to get it and roughly what it costs.

Prices are ballpark and change — check the vendor before you commit a budget line to it.

① Must-have ② First month ③ Later Legal & compliance
1

Must-have before real money moves

Right now checkout runs on a mock payment provider — every GCash/Maya/card charge "succeeds" instantly with no money moving. Nothing below is optional if that's about to change.

Payment gateway
Replaces the mock provider. PayMongo and Xendit both speak GCash, Maya, GrabPay, and cards natively — no monthly fee, you pay per transaction, but going live requires a registered business and a bank account on file.
PayMongo recommended~2.5% e-wallets · ~3.5% + ₱15 cards
Xendit~2.5–3.5% per transaction
DragonPay~₱15–20 flat · adds over-the-counter (7-Eleven, banks)
Business registration
Every PH payment gateway requires this before it will release your live API keys — it's the actual bottleneck, budget a few days for approval.
DTI (sole proprietor)₱200 – ₱2,000
SEC (corporation)₱5,000 – ₱10,000+

🇵🇭 Specific to a PH marketplace

Compliance, not code

These don't live in the repo, but a payment processor or a lawyer will ask about them before real transactions flow.

BIR registrationRequired once you're operating as a real business collecting payment — tax registration and official receipts.
NPC registrationThe National Privacy Commission requires registering data processing systems once you're handling personal and payment data at scale. Free to file, easy to forget.
Seller KYCThe admin approval queue (seller status: pending → approved) already exists in the app — decide what documentation you'll actually check before approving someone.
