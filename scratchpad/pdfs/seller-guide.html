What is ATBP?

"ATBP" is short for at iba pa — Filipino for "and other things," "and so on." It's also exactly how the marketplace works.

ATBP is a Philippine marketplace built around handmade, vintage, pre-loved, collectible, and just plain interesting finds — the kind of things you'd stumble onto at a good weekend bazaar, a flea market table, a maker's booth, or a collector's swap meet. Closets being cleared out. Yard sales going up for the weekend. A one-of-one piece from a local maker. A trading card someone's finally letting go of.

That's a deliberately different shape than a mass-market catalogue marketplace. ATBP isn't optimized for "search a SKU, buy the cheapest one, done." It's built for browsing — for the moment someone finds something they weren't specifically looking for, from someone whose shop they'll want to come back to.

"Find something different."

Which is why ATBP is built around seller identity, not anonymous listings. Every shop on ATBP has a real presence:

Shop & Story — your own storefront page, banner, logo, and description
Products — organized under your shop, not lost in a generic grid
Reviews & ratings — buyer feedback tied to real completed orders
Followers — buyers who follow your shop see your new listings
Badges — Verified Seller, Verified Maker, Rising Seller, Highly Rated, Fast Shipper, Local Seller, and more
Collections — a chance to be hand-picked into "ATBP Picks," a curated spotlight

Buyers come to ATBP to discover — through Home, Explore, Trending, Deals, and Local — and what they discover is your shop, not just a product row.

Who probably shouldn't sell on ATBP

We'd rather tell you upfront: ATBP isn't built for pure commodity resale at the lowest possible price with no shop identity — that's a different kind of marketplace, optimized for a different kind of shopper. It's also not the place for anything on our Prohibited Items list (weapons, illegal drugs, counterfeit goods, live animals, unregistered prescription medicines, and a handful of other regulated categories), and it's not a fit if you'd rather list-and-forget than build a shop people can follow and come back to — discovery on ATBP rewards presence, not just inventory.

The honest version: if what you're selling has a story — who made it, where it's been, why it's rare, why it's yours to sell — ATBP is built for that story to matter.

Why sell on ATBP?

Real reasons — not the biggest-marketplace pitch, because that's not what ATBP is.

ATBP is younger and smaller than Shopee, Lazada, or TikTok Shop. We won't pretend otherwise — and we won't claim to send you more traffic than they can, or to be flatly cheaper than every platform in every scenario. What ATBP offers instead is a marketplace built specifically around discovery and identity, not raw transaction volume:

A discovery-first homepage. Browse by Vibe, Trending Now, curated ATBP Picks, and Deals — buyers land on the site to browse, the way they'd wander a bazaar.
A real storefront, not a listing ID. Banner, bio, badges, ratings, and a follower count that's actually yours.
Followers who come back. Buyers follow shops they like and see new drops without having to search again.
A shot at "ATBP Picks." Our curated collections spotlight are hand-picked, not pay-to-play.
Local discovery. Buyers can find nearby sellers, closets, yard sales, and markets specifically — not buried in a national catalogue.
Formats built for what you actually sell. Closets and Yard Sales are their own destinations, not squeezed into a generic "used goods" category.

And, plainly: ATBP is a chance to reach buyers who are specifically looking for something different — outside whatever social audience you've already built.

Payment processing

GCash, Maya, and QR Ph carry no fee beyond your plan's commission. Card and Online Banking orders carry one additional flat fee of ₱15 — not a percentage, and it's the same ₱15 whether the order is ₱200 or ₱20,000.

How to create a great listing

The details that actually move a "maybe" to a "buy now."

Photos. Natural light, multiple angles, and — for anything pre-loved or vintage — close-ups of any flaws. Buyers trust what they can see clearly.
Titles. Specific beats clever: item, key material or era, and size or variant if relevant.
Descriptions. Say what it is, what condition it's really in, and what's included. For made-to-order, state your turnaround time up front.
Pricing & stock. Set a real price and accurate quantity — ATBP tracks available stock per listing.
Condition, honestly. ATBP's condition scale runs Brand New → Like New → Excellent → Good → Fair. Under-stating condition costs you disputes; over-stating costs you reviews.
Shipping or pickup. Set what you actually offer — shipping, local pickup, or local delivery — so buyers aren't surprised at checkout.

By category

Vintage & pre-loved: measurements, materials, and any wear — disclosed, not hidden. Collectibles: edition, rarity, and authenticity details if you have them. Handmade: materials and process — buyers on ATBP want to know how it was made. Made-to-order: set expectations on turnaround before someone orders.

Getting your first sales

Practical, ATBP-specific tactics — not generic ecommerce advice.

  • Upload consistently. "Just Added" is a real homepage shelf — regular new listings keep you visible there.
  • Build a shop people recognize. A complete profile with a logo and banner reads as a real shop, not a one-off listing.
  • Share your ATBP storefront link on your existing social channels — bring your audience to a shop they can follow and buy from directly.
  • Encourage follows. Followers see your new listings without having to search for you again.
  • Try Boost or Featured placements if it makes sense for a launch — self-serve, starting around ₱99–149 per listing.
  • Respond quickly to messages and orders — it shows up in your seller stats and your reputation.
  • Ask happy buyers for a review. Reviews and ratings are visible on your shop page and build trust for the next buyer.
  • Keep new products coming. A shop that updates regularly earns more repeat visits than one that lists once and goes quiet.

Seller FAQ

The questions we actually get.

How and when do I get paid?
From Studio → Payouts, once you have an available balance, out to bank, GCash, or Maya. Standard payouts process in the ordinary queue; instant payout is available for a flat ₱99 fee if you need funds right away.
What happens with returns or a buyer dispute?
ATBP handles this through a dispute process rather than a separate "returns" flow — a buyer or seller can raise one, and it's reviewed and resolved (refund approved or denied) from there.
What are seller badges?
Recognitions like Verified Seller, Verified Maker, Rising Seller, Highly Rated, Fast Shipper, Local Seller, and Founding Seller, shown on your shop page as you qualify for them.
How do followers work?
Buyers can follow your shop from your storefront page and get surfaced your new listings — no extra setup needed on your end.
Can I promote my listings?
Yes — self-serve Boost, Featured, Category Feature, Homepage Feature, and Sponsored Campaign placements are available from Studio, priced per placement.
How do Deals work?
You set a scheduled sale price and window on your own listing — it's automatically live during that window and shows up on the Deals page.
Can I run an auction?
Auctions are fully built into ATBP but temporarily turned off site-wide while we build up a large enough buyer base for bidding to feel active. They'll switch back on as that grows.
What about Closets, Yard Sales, and Made-to-Order?
Each is its own listing format with its own discovery page — Closets and Yard Sales for personal pre-loved selling, Made-to-Order for custom pieces built after a buyer orders.
What can't I sell?
See the full Prohibited Items policy — weapons, illegal drugs, counterfeit goods, unregistered prescription medicines, live animals, and a short list of other regulated categories.
How do I close my shop?
Contact ATBP Support — they'll make sure any pending orders are settled first before closing your store.
