01 — The Concept

A marketplace built for browsing, not just searching.

ATBP ("at iba pa" — Filipino for "and other things") is a Philippine marketplace focused on discovery rather than mass-market commodity shopping. Sellers list their own products; ATBP provides the marketplace, discovery, storefronts, transactions, and tools.

🎨 Handmade & Art

Makers and artists, one-of-ones and small batches

🕰️ Vintage & Pre-Loved

Closets, yard sales, and personal resale

🧸 Collectibles

Trading cards, toys, anime, gaming, vinyl

✨ Made-to-Order

Custom pieces built after a buyer orders

Plus a small set of adjacent formats: shelf-stable food & snacks, remote services, and digital products.

02 — The Problem

Not every shopper is searching for a SKU.

The largest Philippine marketplaces are optimized for search, price, convenience, and massive catalogues — genuinely well-suited to commodity shopping. But there's a different, well-documented shopping behavior they're not built around:

"I want to browse — find something I wasn't specifically looking for."

"I want something with personality, made by someone I can follow."

"I want to support a small seller, not a warehouse."

92% of Filipino survey respondents say they buy secondhand goods — among the highest rates surveyed in Southeast Asia — and the Philippines' secondhand apparel market alone is estimated to grow from $5.2B (2025) to $17.9B by 2031 (22.8% CAGR). That behavior doesn't have a dedicated home on a mass-market catalogue platform.

Sources: Carousell/WEF secondhand-consumption survey (Southeast Asia); Mobility Foresights, Philippines Second-hand Apparel Market Size and Forecasts, checked September 2026.

08 — Business Model

Simple, transparent, transaction-aligned.

Commission

10% standard, 8% on the Premium seller plan — ATBP's primary revenue line, earned only when a seller earns.

Subscriptions

Pro ₱699/mo and Premium ₱1,999/mo unlock higher listing limits and seller tools.

Promotions & ads

Self-serve seller placements (Boost, Featured, Homepage) plus admin-managed third-party advertising.

Transaction fees

A flat ₱15 processing fee (Card/Online Banking) and an opt-in, buyer-paid Buyer Protection Fee.

No cost of goods, no inventory risk — ATBP's cost structure is platform infrastructure and people, not merchandise. The fee model deliberately mirrors what's simplest for a seller to understand: one flat commission, not a stack of transaction/processing/growth-support line items layered on top.

09 — Where ATBP Is Today

A complete, working product — early in its commercial rollout.

Built & live

Full buyer/seller marketplace flows — discovery, storefronts, checkout, reviews, following, collections, deals, closets, yard sales, local discovery, seller plans, sponsored placements, admin operations tooling, and a Founding Seller program with 200 slots (197 remaining at time of writing).

Built, not yet switched on

Auctions and livestream selling are fully implemented, feature-flagged off until buyer volume can sustain them. A real courier integration (Ninja Van) exists in code, pending production hardening.

What partners should know

Payments, payouts, and subscription billing currently run through a mock provider while ATBP prepares to integrate a live Philippine payment gateway — this is the primary near-term technical milestone, not a gap that affects the product experience today.

10 — Partnership Opportunities

Where a partner plugs in directly.

💳 Payment partners

ATBP's checkout and commission engine are built to a real PSP integration point (GCash, Maya, QR Ph, Card, Online Banking) — the architecture is ready; the integration is the open door.

📦 Logistics partners

A courier-adapter pattern already exists in code (Ninja Van as the reference integration) alongside Manual and Local Pickup fulfillment — built to add another courier without changing checkout.

🎪 Event & market partners

ATBP Markets and community Events are a real, admin-supported feature — a natural fit for organizers of maker markets, bazaars, and pop-ups seeking a digital counterpart.

🏷️ Brand & advertising partners

A working sponsored-placement and third-party advertising system (impressions/clicks tracked) is live at the infrastructure level, ready for a formalized rate card.
